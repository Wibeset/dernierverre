Frequently asked questions

Where are my bar and favourites stored?

+

Only on your iPhone. Dernier Verre has no account and no server, and it doesn’t connect to the internet, so we can’t see them. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or to a computer, they are part of that backup.

How do I move my bar to a new iPhone?

+

Set up the new iPhone from a backup of your old one (iCloud or computer), or transfer directly from the old iPhone during setup. Your bar, favourites and preferences come along with the app. Dernier Verre doesn’t sync between devices through iCloud.

How do I add or remove ingredients in my bar?

+

In the Mon bar tab, tick the bottles and fresh ingredients you have. You can also tap any ingredient in a recipe to add it to your bar or take it out. To start over, tap Tout décocher (untick all).

Why don’t I see a cocktail I’m looking for?

+

Check which drinks the feed shows: Tous (all), Il manque ≤ 1 (missing one at most) or Prêts à faire (ready to make). Then check the spirit you started from and the filters; Réinitialiser (reset) clears occasion, flavour, strength and difficulty.

How do I switch between millilitres and ounces?

+

On any recipe, switch between ml and oz. Dernier Verre remembers your choice, and the + and − buttons scale every quantity from one glass to twelve.

Is the app in English?

+

The app is in French. Every recipe is also on this website in English, with the same measures in ml or oz.

How do I delete my data?

+

Tap Tout décocher in Mon bar to clear your bar, and tap the heart again to remove a favourite. Deleting the app erases all its data from your iPhone.

Is Dernier Verre free?

+

Yes, with all 52 recipes, no ads and no account.
