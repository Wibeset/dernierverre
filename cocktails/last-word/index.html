Gin · Coupe

Last Word

Herbal and intense, in equal parts

Strength

Strong

Difficulty

Intermediate

Method

Shaken

🫒 ApéritifFresh & herbalCitrusSweet

A Last Word is made with 22.5 ml gin, 22.5 ml Green Chartreuse, 22.5 ml maraschino liqueur and 22.5 ml lime juice. Shaken and served in a coupe.

Gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino and lime in equal measure. A Prohibition-era recipe rediscovered by modern bartenders: sweet, sour, herbal and perfectly balanced.

Ingredients

1 glass
  • Gin22.5 ml
  • Green Chartreuse22.5 ml
  • Maraschino liqueur22.5 ml
  • Lime juice22.5 ml

Garnish Maraschino cherry

Preparation

  1. 01Shake with ice.
  2. 02Strain into a chilled coupe.
