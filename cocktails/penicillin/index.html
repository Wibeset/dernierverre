Penicillin

Scotch, honey and ginger: a modern remedy

Strength

Medium

Difficulty

Intermediate

Method

Shaken

🔥 Cosy nightSpicedCitrusSweet

A Penicillin is made with 60 ml scotch, 25 ml lemon juice and 25 ml honey-ginger syrup. Shaken and served in an old fashioned glass.

A whisky sour sweetened with honey-ginger syrup. Created in New York in the 2000s, it's warming, spicy and soothing — the bartender's cure-all.

Ingredients

1 glass
  • Scotch60 ml
  • Lemon juice25 ml
  • Honey-ginger syrup25 ml

Garnish Candied ginger

Preparation

  1. 01Shake with ice.
  2. 02Strain over a large ice cube.
