Cucumber-basil lemonade

A spa-day lemonade, herbal and refreshing

Strength

Alcohol-free

Difficulty

Easy

Method

Shaken

☀️ Patio🥐 BrunchFresh & herbalCitrus

A Cucumber-basil lemonade is made with 30 ml lemon juice, 20 ml simple syrup, 3 slices of cucumber and 4 leaves of fresh basil, topped with soda water. Shaken and served in a highball glass.

Cucumber and basil muddled with lemon and topped with soda. Alcohol-free, garden-fresh and grown-up enough for the whole table.

Ingredients

1 glass
  • Lemon juice30 ml
  • Simple syrup20 ml
  • Cucumber3 slices
  • Fresh basil4 leaves
  • Soda waterTop up

Garnish Cucumber ribbon

Preparation

  1. 01Muddle the cucumber and basil in the shaker.
  2. 02Add the lemon, syrup and ice, then shake.
  3. 03Strain over ice and top up with soda water.
