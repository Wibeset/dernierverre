Sidecar

Cognac and citrus, Roaring Twenties elegance

Strength

Strong

Difficulty

Easy

Method

Shaken

🫒 Apéritif🌙 NightcapCitrusDry & strong

A Sidecar is made with 50 ml cognac, 20 ml triple sec and 20 ml lemon juice. Shaken and served in a coupe.

Cognac, orange liqueur and lemon, served in a sugar-rimmed coupe. A Jazz Age classic that's tart, rich and polished.

Ingredients

1 glass
  • Cognac50 ml
  • Triple sec (Cointreau)20 ml
  • Lemon juice20 ml
  • SugaroptionalTo rim the glass

Garnish Orange zest

Preparation

  1. 01Rim the coupe with sugar (optional).
  2. 02Shake with ice.
  3. 03Strain into the coupe.
