Alcohol-free · Highball glass

Virgin paloma

Grapefruit, lime and a pinch of salt

Strength

Alcohol-free

Difficulty

Easy

Method

Built

☀️ Patio🫒 ApéritifCitrusBitterSparkling

A Virgin paloma is made with 90 ml grapefruit juice, 15 ml lime juice and 10 ml agave syrup, topped with soda water. Built in the glass and served in a highball glass.

The paloma without the tequila: grapefruit, lime and a touch of agave, lifted by a pinch of salt and soda. Proof that alcohol-free doesn't mean boring.

Ingredients

1 glass
  • Grapefruit juice90 ml
  • Lime juice15 ml
  • Agave syrup10 ml
  • Soda waterTop up
  • Saltoptional1 pinch

Garnish Grapefruit slice

Preparation

  1. 01Fill the glass with ice.
  2. 02Add the juices, agave and salt, then top up with soda water.
