Dry martini

Dry, cold and elegant

Strength

Strong

Difficulty

Intermediate

Method

Stirred

🫒 ApéritifDry & strong

A Dry martini is made with 60 ml gin and 10 ml dry vermouth. Stirred and served in a martini glass.

Gin, a whisper of dry vermouth and a lot of stirring. The dry martini is about temperature and restraint: keep everything ice cold and serve it right away.

Ingredients

1 glass
  • Gin60 ml
  • Dry vermouth10 ml
  • Orange bittersoptional1 dash

Garnish Olive or lemon zest

Preparation

  1. 01Stir the ingredients for 30 seconds in a mixing glass filled with ice.
  2. 02Strain into a well-chilled martini glass.

Keep it in your pocket.
