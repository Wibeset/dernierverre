Moscow mule

Vodka, ginger and lime

Strength

Light

Difficulty

Easy

Method

Built

☀️ Patio🎉 PartySpicedCitrusSparkling

Vodka, lime and spicy ginger beer in a frosty copper mug. Crisp, zesty and as easy to make as it is to drink.

Ingredients

1 glass
  • Vodka50 ml
  • Lime juice15 ml
  • Ginger beer120 ml

Garnish Lime wedge and mint

Preparation

  1. 01Fill the mug with ice.
  2. 02Pour in the vodka and lime.
  3. 03Top up with ginger beer.
