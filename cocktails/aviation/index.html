Gin · Coupe

Aviation

Floral, the colour of the sky

Strength

Medium

Difficulty

Advanced

Method

Shaken

🫒 ApéritifCitrusFruity

Gin, lemon and maraschino, tinted pale lilac by a touch of crème de violette. A pre-Prohibition New York classic that tastes as delicate as it looks.

Ingredients

1 glass
  • Gin50 ml
  • Maraschino liqueur10 ml
  • Crème de violette5 ml
  • Lemon juice15 ml

Garnish Maraschino cherry

Preparation

  1. 01Shake with ice.
  2. 02Strain into a chilled coupe.

Keep it in your pocket.
