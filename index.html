01 — How it works

1, 2, shake

From bottle to glass in three moves.

No sign-up, no setup. Open the app and the first poster is already waiting.

01

Pick a spirit

Start from gin, rum, tequila — or nothing at all. The whole feed repaints itself in the colour of each drink.

02

Tick your bar

Check off the bottles and fresh things you have. Recipes you can make right now float to the top.

03

Mix it

Exact measures in ml or oz, scaled from one glass to twelve, and numbered steps you can follow with sticky fingers.

02 — My bar

Try it here

What’s in your bar? Tick it.

This is the real recipe library from the app. Tap what you have and see what you can serve right now.

03 — Features

Nothing you won’t use

Built like a poster. Pours like a pro.

Big type you can read across the kitchen counter, and only the controls you actually reach for.

N° 01

The poster feed

One drink per screen, full-bleed in its own colour. Swipe up for the next idea.

N° 02

“Ready to serve” and “one ingredient away”, worked out from the bottles you tick.

Occasion, flavour, strength, difficulty. Apéritif on the patio? Something smoky for a cosy night?

Switch between ml and oz, and go from one glass to twelve. Every quantity follows.

05 — Frequently asked

FAQ

Is Dernier Verre free?

+

Yes. Dernier Verre will be free to download on the App Store for iPhone, with all 52 recipes included. It’s coming soon.

When is Dernier Verre available?

+

Soon. The app is in its final stretch before launch on the App Store for iPhone. In the meantime, every recipe is on this site.

Do I need an account?

+

No. There’s nothing to sign up for. Your bar and your favorites are saved on your iPhone and never leave it.

Does it work offline?

+

Yes. All 52 recipes are built into the app, so it works at the cottage, on the boat, or in a basement bar with no signal.

What if I’m missing one ingredient?

+

My bar lists the drinks you’re one ingredient away from and says which one. On the poster, the missing ingredient is struck through so you can improvise — or make a grocery run.

Millilitres or ounces?

+

Both. Switch units on any recipe and the app remembers your choice. Quantities scale from one glass to twelve.

Any alcohol-free drinks?

+

Six of them, from a virgin mojito to a cucumber-basil lemonade. Pick “Alcohol-free” as your starting point and the feed shows only those.

Why “Dernier Verre”?

+

It’s French for “last glass” — the one more round you pour when the night is going well. We’d like it to be a good one.
