Dernier Verre has no account and no sign-up. Your bar and your favourites are stored on your iPhone, and we never receive them.

This policy explains what information the Dernier Verre app for iPhone and the dernierverre.app website handle, and why. Dernier Verre is made by Wibeset, in Quebec, Canada.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you set in Dernier Verre is stored on your device:

  • the ingredients you tick in your bar;
  • your favourite cocktails;
  • your preferences, such as millilitres or ounces and which drinks to show (all of them, missing one at most, or ready to make).

We have no server that stores this data, and we cannot see it. Dernier Verre does not sync it with iCloud. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or on a computer, this data is included in that backup, which is how it follows you to a new iPhone.

No internet connection

All 52 recipes are built into the app. Dernier Verre makes no network requests: it doesn’t look anything up, download anything or send anything while you use it, and it works the same in airplane mode.

Sharing a recipe

When you tap the share button on a recipe, Dernier Verre gives its text (ingredients scaled to your number of glasses, steps and garnish) to your iPhone’s share sheet. It goes only to the app or person you choose. We never receive it.

Permissions

Dernier Verre doesn’t ask for access to your camera, photos, contacts, location or microphone, and it never sends notifications.

App analytics and crash reports

Dernier Verre does not include any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools. If you chose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may share with us anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, which help us fix bugs. They do not identify you and do not include your bar or your favourites. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

This website

dernierverre.app has no cookies, analytics, ads or trackers, and its fonts are served from the site itself. It is hosted on GitHub Pages: as with any website, GitHub receives your IP address and basic technical information when you visit, and handles it under the GitHub Privacy Statement. On recipe pages, your choice of ml or oz is remembered in your browser’s local storage; it never leaves your browser.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t ask for your name, email address or any account.
  • We don’t show ads or track you across other apps and websites.
  • We don’t sell or share your information.

Deleting your data

Since your data lives on your iPhone, you control it. Untick an ingredient, or tap Tout décocher (untick all) in Mon bar, to clear your bar, and tap the heart again to remove a favourite. Deleting Dernier Verre removes all its data from your iPhone. Backups you made earlier may still contain it until they are replaced or deleted.

Children

Dernier Verre collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Dernier Verre handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.
